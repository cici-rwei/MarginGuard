**标题：MarginGuard — Retail Risk & Financial Decision Intelligence**

MarginGuard helps small retailers turn daily sales into explainable purchasing decisions. Users can explore a historical Walmart M5 demonstration or analyse their own validated CSV data. Python compares two forecasting baselines over a chronological 14-day holdout, then supports inventory coverage and Wait, Reorder and Discount scenarios. Financial outputs distinguish procurement cash from cost of goods sold and support AUD or USD without currency conversion. An independent ABS workspace provides Australian market context. Uploaded data is temporary, and inventory, costs and supplier terms remain explicit user assumptions. Recommendations compare specified scenarios; they do not guarantee optimal orders or future business outcomes.
