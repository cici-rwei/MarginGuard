How to read this overview

Products means product–store combinations. Products Analysed counts successful forecasts; inventory assessment also needs a complete, correctly dated stock snapshot. Missing information is not zero stock and is not a low-risk result.

Stockout risk uses the existing delivery-time demand rule. Sufficient stock means sufficient for delivery-time demand and backup stock, not necessarily all 14 forecast days. Each combination has one primary risk category; individual warning flags can overlap. Reorder attention is shown separately. Your chosen days-of-cover limit controls the excess stock warning.

Each product can have different forecast dates. This is a prioritised review list, not a combined cash budget, a purchase order or a guarantee.
