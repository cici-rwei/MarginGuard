What this stock assessment means

The risk assessment compares stock with demand during the supplier delivery time and your backup stock target. A sufficient result does not necessarily mean you have enough stock for the full 14-day forecast.

Stock terms and calculation notes

Supplier Delivery Time (lead time) is the number of days before a new order arrives. Backup Stock (safety stock) is the extra stock target you choose for unexpected demand or delays. These are your assumptions.
