04B / INVENTORY & SALES

Official inventory-to-sales ratio

Business Indicators Table 23 · Retail trade · Original.

Official ratio · no risk score
Loading the official ratio…
BUSINESS INSIGHTS

Definition: quarter-end inventory value divided by sales during the quarter, on the same original current-price basis. This is a stock-to-flow ratio, not inventory turnover days or days of cover. It does not measure an individual business's stockout risk. No high, medium or low thresholds are assigned.

Source and interpretation

Australian Bureau of Statistics. Monetary values are nominal AUD; no chain-volume measures are included. Original, Seasonally Adjusted and Trend are separate published series. Missing observations remain unavailable and charts show gaps.

Growth percentages and descriptive Business Insights are calculated in Python from the Phase 4A data snapshot. These descriptions are not forecasts or causal claims. ABS industry indicators do not modify product forecasts or scenario calculations.
