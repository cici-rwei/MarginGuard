What the financial results mean

Revenue is the value of sales you can fulfil. Gross Profit is revenue less the cost of goods actually sold (COGS), before other operating expenses. Gross Margin shows that profit as a percentage of revenue. Procurement Cash is the money needed for the new order, including delivery and other purchasing fees; it is not the same as the cost of stock sold.

Unmet Demand is forecast demand that stock cannot fulfil. A strategy marked Not feasible breaks a stated budget, minimum order or other constraint and is not eligible for recommendation.

Details: delivery timing, costs and discount assumptions

New stock arrives at the start of day lead-time + 1. Existing stock sells first. Procurement cash is separate from COGS. Discount uplift is a user assumption, not validated price elasticity. Prior outstanding orders are not included in these simulations.
