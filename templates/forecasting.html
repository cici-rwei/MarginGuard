How these forecasts work

7-day Moving Average
The last seven observed days are averaged once and repeated across the horizon.

Same-Weekday Average
Each weekday uses its four occurrences in the preceding 28 observed days, frozen at the forecast origin.

The final 14 observed days are held out. Lower MAE selects the model; an exact tie favors the moving average. The selected model then uses all observations for the separate future forecast. MAE is measured in units/day.
