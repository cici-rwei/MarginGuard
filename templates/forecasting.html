Understanding forecast error

Average Forecast Error (MAE) is the average absolute difference between predicted and actual daily sales, measured in units per day. Lower is better; it does not measure future certainty.

How the two methods and historical backtest work

7-day Moving Average
The last seven observed days are averaged once and repeated across the horizon.

Same-Weekday Average
Each weekday uses its four occurrences in the preceding 28 observed days, frozen at the forecast origin.

In the historical backtest, the final 14 observed days are held out. Lower MAE selects the model; an exact tie favors the moving average. The selected model then uses all observations for the separate future forecast. MAE is measured in units/day.
